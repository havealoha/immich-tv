## Overview

Immich TV is a TV-first client for connecting to your self-hosted Immich server and browsing your photo and video library. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, what may be stored on your device, and what is not collected by the developer.

## Information the App Accesses

When you use Immich TV, the app may access:

- Your Immich server URL
- Your Immich account email address
- Your Immich account password during sign-in
- Your Immich account access token returned by the Immich server
- Your Immich profile information returned by the Immich server, such as your name, email address, and user ID
- Photo, video, album, favorites, and related media metadata made available by your Immich server

## How the Information Is Used

Immich TV uses this information only to:

- Connect to the Immich server you choose
- Authenticate your sign-in
- Display your media library, albums, favorites, and slideshow content
- Restore saved profiles on your device if you choose to save them
- Improve the in-app browsing experience through local image prefetching and caching

## Information Stored on Your Device

If you choose to save a profile on your device, Immich TV may store:

- Profile name
- Email address
- Server URL and related server configuration
- Last-used timestamp
- A 4-digit profile PIN
- Your Immich password for saved-profile sign-in

Immich TV stores profile metadata using local device preferences and stores saved credentials and PIN data using secure device storage where available.

This stored information is used only to let you re-open a saved profile on that device.

## Information Sent Over the Network

Immich TV sends data only to:

- The Immich server that you configure
- Media URLs provided by that Immich server for thumbnails, images, and video playback

Immich TV does not send your personal data to the developer for app analytics, marketing, advertising, or profiling.

## What the Developer Does Not Collect

Khondakar Afridi does not knowingly collect, store, sell, rent, or share your personal data through a separate developer-operated backend for Immich TV.

In particular, Immich TV does not intentionally collect:

- Advertising identifiers for ad targeting
- Third-party analytics profiles
- Location data for tracking
- Contact lists
- Messages
- Call logs

## Third-Party Services

Immich TV relies on your own Immich server and platform-provided storage mechanisms on your device. Your use of Immich TV may also involve third-party components provided by the operating system or Flutter packages, such as secure storage, local preferences, and media playback.

Those components are used to provide core app functionality.

## Data Retention

Data saved by Immich TV remains on your device until:

- You remove the saved profile within the app, if such functionality is available
- You clear the app's data
- You uninstall the app

Data available from your Immich server is controlled by you and the server you operate or access.

## Security

Immich TV attempts to store saved credentials using secure device storage where supported. However, no software or storage method can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for the security of:

- The device running the app
- The Immich server you connect to
- Your network environment
- The password and PIN you choose

## Children's Privacy

Immich TV is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated service.

## Your Choices

You can choose:

- Whether to sign in to an Immich server
- Whether to save a profile on the device
- Whether to uninstall the app or clear app storage

If you do not want local profile data stored, do not use the saved-profile feature.
